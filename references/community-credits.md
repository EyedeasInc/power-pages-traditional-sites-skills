# Sources & community credits

The knowledge in this plugin's `references/` is grounded first in **Microsoft Learn** and
cross-checked against Microsoft's **official Power Pages plugin**, then enriched with field-tested
insight from the Power Pages community. Community techniques are paraphrased (never copied) and
labelled as practitioner notes; where community advice conflicts with Microsoft, Microsoft's guidance
wins and the correction is stated in the reference.
